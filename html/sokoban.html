unfinished:Sokoban

Push all the barrels onto the target squares.

Use the arrow keys to move the player around the grid. You can push a barrel by walking into it (as long as the square on the far side of the barrel is empty). You cannot pull barrels, and you cannot push more than one barrel at a time.

Target squares are shown as circular markings on the floor. The puzzle is complete when every barrel is resting on a target square.
