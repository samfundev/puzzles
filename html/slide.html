unfinished:Slide

Slide the blocks around the grid to move the blue block to the target position marked in green.

Click and drag a block to move it. Blocks can only slide into empty spaces or over force fields (shown with a grid pattern), which only the blue main block can cross. Each block can slide horizontally or vertically as far as possible in one move until it hits a wall or another block.

The goal is to move the blue main block to completely cover the green target area in as few moves as possible.
